Property Management Company in Japan for Foreign Landlords

If I were buying a Japanese rental property from overseas, I would ask a property management company exactly what it would handle, how it would report to me, and which charges sit outside its monthly fee. For initial planning, I would use 3–5% of monthly rent as a figure for standard full management, then get a written quote for the property.

In an FY2025 survey of rental management companies published in 2026, Japan’s Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism found that about 80% charged a percentage of rent. The most common band was 3% to under 5%, reported by 51.9% of companies, followed by 5% to under 10%, reported by 38.8%. A survey band is a starting point; the contract tells me what I would actually pay and receive.

What I would ask a management company to handle

I would ask for a written list of services, including how the company deals with tenant communication, rent collection, maintenance requests and problems at the property. I would also ask which decisions it can make without my approval and when it needs to contact me.

I would not assume every company includes the same work in “full management.” If a service matters to me, I would want it named in the agreement.

How I would compare fees

I would ask each company for its management fee, what that fee covers and a list of charges that may be billed separately. I would also ask how the fee is calculated and what happens to the fee if the property is vacant.

The 3–5% planning figure helps me start a budget, but I would use the company’s written quote when assessing a particular property. I would ask the brokerage or management company to explain any charge I do not understand.

Questions for an overseas landlord

Managing from abroad makes communication especially important. I would ask:

  • Can the company communicate with me in English, and will the agreement be available in English?
  • How often will it send reports, and what information will they include?
  • How will it contact me about repairs and obtain approval for spending?
  • How will rent and expense statements be provided?
  • What notice and conditions apply if I want to end the agreement?

I would also ask a qualified professional what records I should keep and what tax obligations apply to me. I would check the answers for my own circumstances before signing.

FAQ

How much should I budget for property management in Japan?

I would start with 3–5% of monthly rent for standard full management, then request a written quote and check which services and extra charges it includes.

Does every management company charge a percentage of rent?

No. The MLIT survey found that about 80% of surveyed companies did. I would ask each company how it calculates its fee.

Can an overseas owner rely on English-language service?

I would check this with each company. I would ask whether day-to-day communication, reports and the agreement are available in English before choosing a manager.
